I've been building Loot, a personal finance app for South Africans.

The idea is simple: your bank balance includes money that's already spoken for (rent, the car, debit orders). Loot works out the number that's actually yours: available to spend this month and per day until payday.

What's in it:
• Summary: your real number, every day
• Can I afford it?: Comfortable, Tight or Not recommended, worked out from your real numbers
• Salary planner and a Tax centre that uses SARS brackets
• Statements, goals and a Loot Score for money habits
• Runs on the web and installs to your home screen

Know your number before you spend it.
