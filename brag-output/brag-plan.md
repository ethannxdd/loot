# Loot — brag plan

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080 · **Length:** 22s · **Tone:** `app-store` leaning `polished` (inferred — it's a calm, Apple-flavoured money app, not a joke) · **Music:** original synth bed at 120 BPM (beat = 0.5s, bar = 2s) · **SFX:** on (bundled Pixabay library) · **Voice:** off

## Rubric

1. **What is it?** A South African personal finance app (web + installable PWA) that tells you what's actually yours to spend this month.
2. **Who's it for?** Salaried South Africans who get paid monthly/fortnightly and want to stop guessing.
3. **The one thing:** Your bank balance includes money that's already spoken for. Loot shows the real number.
4. **Best visual proof:** the Summary hero — R 6,883 available to spend, R 229/day, the available/committed/saving bar.
5. **Second proof:** "Can I afford it?" — a straight verdict (Comfortable / Tight / Not recommended) from your real numbers.
6. **Third proof:** it's built for SA — rands, SARS tax brackets in the Tax centre, salary planner, statement import — and it looks like an iPhone app (Liquid Glass tab bar).
7. **Real copy to use:** "Know your loot." · "Available to spend" · "Can I afford it?" · "Know your number before you spend it."
8. **Hook:** Numbers from the demo account: bank says R 29,800; R 22,917 is committed; R 6,883 is actually yours. The hook *is* the product insight.
9. **Banned:** "streamline", "all-in-one", "take control of your finances", stock people.
